## 多流长输入观察(190K 级 ×8)

- 冷请求:墙钟 261 s,prefill 交错期各流 decode 5–6 tok/s;PP 的 prefill/decode 交错调度使混合负载延迟可感。
- 同语料重复请求(缓存命中):墙钟 19 s;命中场景业务收益大。
- 8 路中 1 路简单 needle 未命中(单流无此现象),原因未定,需对照实验。

## 工程记录

- PCIe Gen2 x4 为本机稳态(多次冷断电确认),TP4 官方 x16 数字在本机不可达是预期行为。
- marlin 权重转换期间 reserved 显存攀升而 allocated 平坦(碎片化),1.4.x 需模块级 GC 缓解;1.6.0 已不需要。
- 兩輪 482K 測試期間 XID=0,無異常。

## English summary

Historical record on engine 1.4.x (378c37b00), kept for the layout trade-off evidence. Single-stream decode ranked TP4 (186.5 structured) > TP2+PP2 (163.5) > PP4 (139.4); TP2+PP2 wins on neither axis and was dropped. PP4 carried the long-context crown: first 512K-class pass with ~482K-token prompts at 5,596–5,619 tok/s effective prefill, 130.9–136.5 tok/s generation (two runs, needle hits), KV pool 3,065,297. Multi-stream long-input observations: cold batches starve decode during interleaved prefill (261 s wall for 8×165K), cache-hit repeats drop to 19 s, and 1 of 8 streams missed a simple needle check (single-stream unaffected, cause undetermined).
